Privacy Policy for MealPulse AI

Effective Date: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the MealPulse AI application for mobile devices and related services operated by Gaspare ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our").

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This includes:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Application usage statistics, time spent on pages, and feature interaction.
  • Meal photo plate uploads voluntarily provided for AI calorie and macro estimation.
  • Email address and basic profile info provided during account registration/login.

2. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Processing

MealPulse AI utilizes advanced Vision AI technologies (including Google Gemini AI and OpenAI GPT-4o Vision models) to recognize meal plate photos, estimate food portions, and calculate nutrition macros. Photos submitted for meal scanning are processed in real-time solely to return nutrition insights and are not used to train public AI models without consent.

3. Health & Fitness Data Policy (Health Connect, Physical Activity, Nutrition & Biometrics)

Health Data Commitment: MealPulse AI handles health and fitness data with the highest level of privacy and strict adherence to Google Play Health Content and Services Policies and Health Connect Developer Policies.

Our application accesses, collects, and processes the following health, fitness, and nutrition data to provide personalized calorie and weight management services:

  • Physical Activity & Fitness Data: Daily step count, active energy burned (calories burned), and exercise duration accessed via on-device sensors or health frameworks (such as Android Health Connect and Apple HealthKit).
  • Nutrition & Weight Management Data: Logged food items, estimated portions, calorie intake, macronutrients (proteins, carbohydrates, fats), micronutrients, water consumption, and fasting logs.
  • Biometric Data: Body weight, height, age, gender, activity level, and target weight goals used strictly for calculating Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).

Purposes of Use: Health and fitness data is processed exclusively for the primary functionality of the application: to calculate your daily net calorie deficit, display real-time nutritional progress rings, adjust meal targets, and help you achieve your personal nutrition and weight goals.

Strict Privacy Guarantees on Health Data:

  • No Advertising Use: Health and fitness data is NEVER used for advertising, marketing, or interest-based retargeting.
  • No Data Sales: We NEVER sell, rent, or transfer health data to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.
  • Limited Sharing: Health data is only transmitted securely to our cloud backend (Supabase) to synchronize your personal logs across your devices.
  • User Control: You can grant or revoke health permissions (such as Health Connect access) at any time through your device settings. You can also delete any meal or health entry directly within the app.

4. Medical & Healthcare Advice Disclaimer

Important Medical Disclaimer: MealPulse AI is an informational wellness and fitness tool, not a medical device or healthcare provider.

The information, calorie estimations, nutrition scores, and macro calculations provided by MealPulse AI are intended solely for general fitness, wellness, and educational purposes. MealPulse AI does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or prevention of any disease or medical condition. Always consult a qualified physician, registered dietitian, or certified healthcare provider before starting any new diet, nutritional program, fasting protocol, or vigorous exercise routine.
